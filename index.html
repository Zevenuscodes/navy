Navayu Care - Ayurvedic Clinic

Navayu Care is a dedicated Ayurvedic Health and Wellness Centre focused on promoting natural wellness through traditional Ayurvedic principles and therapeutic practices. We specialize in Nadi Pariksha (Ayurvedic Pulse Examination), Panchakarma therapies, and Kshar Sutra treatment for Piles, Fissure, and Fistula.

Our centre also offers traditional Ayurvedic therapeutic procedures, including Viddha Karma, Agnikarma, Leech Therapy (Jalauka Chikitsa), and Cupping Therapy, with an emphasis on individualized care and holistic well-being.

At Navayu Care, we aim to provide a professional and patient-focused environment where traditional Ayurvedic knowledge meets a personalized approach to healthcare. Our services are designed to support wellness.
