Tridosha

Three doshas. One constitution that is yours alone.

Your prakriti is the balance of Vata, Pitta and Kapha you were born with. Vikriti is where that balance stands today. Treatment closes the gap between the two.

वात

Vata

Air + Space

Governs movement: breath, circulation, nerve impulses. Out of balance it shows as dry skin, irregular sleep, anxiety and joint stiffness.

पित्त

Pitta

Fire + Water

Governs transformation: digestion, metabolism, body heat. Excess appears as acidity, inflammation, skin rashes and irritability.

कफ

Kapha

Earth + Water

Governs structure and lubrication: tissue, immunity, stamina. Aggravated Kapha brings congestion, weight gain and lethargy.

Panchakarma · पञ्चकर्म

The five cleansing actions

Panchakarma removes accumulated doshas from the body. It is preceded by purvakarma (oleation and sudation) and followed by samsarjana krama, a graded return to normal diet.

1

Vamana

वमन

Therapeutic emesis to clear excess Kapha from the chest and stomach.

2

Virechana

विरेचन

Controlled purgation that eliminates Pitta through the intestines.

3

Basti

बस्ति

Medicated enemas of oil or decoction, the primary treatment for Vata.

4

Nasya

नस्य

Herbal oils administered through the nose for head, sinus and neck conditions.

5

Raktamokshana

रक्तमोक्षण

Bloodletting, used selectively for certain skin and blood disorders.

Your vaidya selects which karmas are appropriate. Most programmes use two or three, never all five, and only after a fitness assessment.
